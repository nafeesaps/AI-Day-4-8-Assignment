Product FAQ

What is the company's main product?

The company provides a cloud-based productivity platform designed to help small teams organize their work, manage tasks, and collaborate.

How do customers access the product?

Customers can access the platform through a supported web browser using their registered account credentials.

Can customers use the product remotely?

Yes. The platform is designed for teams that work from different locations. Customers only need a supported browser and an internet connection.

How can a customer report a problem?

Customers can contact the support team through the company's support channel. They should describe the problem clearly and include their order ID when the issue is related to a specific purchase.

Can customers request a refund?

Yes. Eligible customers can request a refund according to the company's refund policy. The standard refund window is 14 days from the original purchase date.

What should customers do if they cannot access their account?

Customers should use the approved account recovery process. They should never send their password or verification code to the support team.

How can customers check an order?

Customers can contact the support service with their order ID. The support system can then retrieve the current order status and expected delivery information.
