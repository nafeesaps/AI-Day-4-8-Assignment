Company Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of the original purchase date. Refund requests submitted after the 14-day refund window are normally not eligible for a standard refund.

To request a refund, customers should provide their order ID and briefly explain the reason for the request. The support team will review the order and confirm whether it meets the refund requirements.

Eligible refunds are normally returned to the original payment method used for the purchase. Processing time may vary depending on the customer's payment provider.

Products that have been substantially used, intentionally damaged, or altered by the customer may not qualify for a refund. If a product arrives damaged or defective, the customer should contact support as soon as possible and provide relevant details or photographs when requested.

The refund policy applies to eligible purchases made through the company's official ordering system. Promotional or special-case purchases may have additional conditions that are communicated to the customer at the time of purchase.

The support team will review unusual refund cases individually. Customers should not send payment information, passwords, or other sensitive credentials when submitting a refund request.

For questions about an existing order, customers should provide only the order ID needed to locate the purchase.
