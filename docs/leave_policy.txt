Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave during each calendar year. Annual leave should normally be requested at least three working days before the planned absence. Employees should submit their leave request through the company's internal leave system and wait for approval before making final plans.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward for up to five days into the following year. Any unused days above this limit will normally expire at the end of the calendar year.

Employees who are sick should inform their manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. For absences lasting more than three consecutive working days, the employee may be asked to provide appropriate medical documentation.

Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected personal or family situation requires immediate attention. The manager will review emergency leave requests based on the circumstances.

Managers are responsible for approving leave while ensuring that sufficient team coverage is maintained. Employees should avoid booking overlapping leave with critical project deadlines unless the manager has approved the arrangement in advance.

All leave requests are subject to business requirements and manager approval.
