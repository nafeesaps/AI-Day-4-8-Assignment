Working Hours and Holidays

The company's standard working hours are Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Employees are expected to remain available during their scheduled working hours unless they are on approved leave or have made an alternative arrangement with their manager.

Because the company operates remotely, employees should maintain reasonable availability for meetings and collaboration during working hours. Team members may have different schedules when required by their role or by coordination with colleagues in other time zones.

The company observes public holidays applicable to its designated operating calendar. Employees will receive the holiday calendar at the beginning of the year or through the company's internal communication system.

When a public holiday falls during an employee's approved annual leave, the treatment of that day will follow the company's leave rules and applicable employment requirements.

Employees who need to work outside their normal schedule should coordinate with their manager in advance whenever possible.

Team meetings may be scheduled during common working hours to support collaboration. Employees should keep their calendars updated and notify relevant team members when they are unavailable.

Questions about working schedules, public holidays, or special working arrangements should be directed to the employee's manager or HR team.
